## Online Assessment (OA)

- **Platform**: HackerEarth
- **Duration**: 1.5 Hours
- **Number of Questions**: 30–40 MCQs
- **Format**: Timed sections, no negative marking.

### Structure & Topics
The OA consisted solely of multiple-choice questions—to my surprise, there were no direct coding questions. The timed sections covered:
- **Probability & Statistics**
- **Finance Concepts**
- **Python & OOPs**
- **AI & Machine Learning**

Some of these questions were repeated from the Barclays BIIC Mentorship test held a few months prior.

### Strategy & Tips
- Since there was no negative marking, make sure to attempt every question.
- Track section timers diligently; don't get stuck on a single problem and solve high-confidence questions first.
- I was targeting the **AI/ML role specifically**. For preparation:
  - Prof. Nipun Batra's slides from the *Introduction to Machine Learning* course were immensely helpful.
  - Review *Top 50 ML Interview Questions*, *Data Science Questions*, *Python Questions*, and *OOPs Questions* on GeeksforGeeks prior to the test and interview.

## Interview Round 1 — Technical & Resume Deep-Dive (~45 Minutes)

- **Platform**: In-Person / Offline
- **Duration**: ~45 minutes

The round began with my personal introduction, followed by a comprehensive walk-through of the projects on my resume.

### Behavioral & Engineering Context
The interviewer asked a blend of technical-behavioral questions grounded directly in real scenarios from my projects:
- Instances where I used AI for task automation.
- How I handled a major mistake in a project and resolved it.
- Experiences leading technical teams.
- Key engineering challenges faced when building AI-centric systems.
- Handling operational logistics, compute, and API costs in AI projects.

### Core Machine Learning Fundamentals
The interviewer tested core conceptual depth:
- **Decision Trees**: Splitting criteria, pruning, bias-variance balance.
- **Data Imbalance**: Handling skewed distributions.
- **Boosting**: Ensembling mechanics and gradient boosting.
- **Regularization (L1 vs. L2)**:
  - Advantages of L1 over L2 regularization (sparsity) and mathematical justification.
  - How to compute the differential/subgradient of the L1 regularization penalty.
- **Activation Functions & Optimization**:
  - Gradient descent convergence, vanishing gradients, MLP architectures.
- **Decision Boundaries**:
  - The interviewer drew several 2D data distributions (including the **XOR distribution**) and asked me to draw the exact decision boundaries a model would construct.

### Python & OOPs
- Python language fundamentals: **lambda functions**, **list comprehensions**, and basic object-oriented principles.
- **Finance Alignment**: When asked about my background in finance, I was transparent that my finance background was nascent at the time, but emphasized my strong interest in applying AI/ML to financial technologies.
