## Technical Questions & Discussion

The interview focused on modern C++ programming mechanics, discrete mathematics, and probability puzzles.

### 1. C++ Template Metaprogramming
- **Discussion**:
  - Explained the use, mechanics, and design patterns of **templates in C++**.
  - Covered compile-time polymorphism, generic data structures, function templates, and template specialization.
  - My primary language wasn't CPP and I was out of practice.

### 2. Number Theory / Primality Testing
- **Problem**:
  - *Is $1027$ a prime number or composite?*
- **Discussion**:
  - Approached primality verification through divisibility testing up to $\lfloor\sqrt{1027}\rfloor \approx 32$.
  - Checked small primes ($2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, \dots$): testing divisibility by $13$ gives $1027 = 13 \times 79$, showing it is composite.

### 3. Probability & Dynamics Puzzle: Infected Ants on a Stick
- **Problem Statement**:
  - There are $15$ ants placed along a one-dimensional stick.
  - Each ant can start moving in either direction (left or right) with equal probability at constant speed.
  - When two ants collide, they bounce off each other and reverse their directions.
  - The middle ant (ant #8) is infected. Whenever an infected ant collides with another ant, the infection transmits to that ant as well.
  - *What is the expected number of ants that will be infected by the time all ants have fallen off the stick?*
